# Sales Performance Analysis -Power BI Project

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze and visualize sales data in order to assess overall business performance, identify top-performing sales representatives, regions, and product categories, understand customer behavior and sales channel effectiveness, and uncover trends that support data driven decision making and strategic planning.

## Dataset Used

<a href="https://github.com/SuccyMoneks/Sales-Performance-Project/blob/main/Sales%20Data.xlsx"></a>

## Key KPIs Displayed

   -  Total Sales
   -  Total Quantity Sold
   -  Total Sales by Region
   -  Number of Sales Representatives

## Business Questions Answered by the Dashboard
1. Which sales representatives contribute the most to total sales?
2. How do sales vary across different regions?
3. What are the monthly sales trends throughout the year?
4. How do sales differ by customer type (New vs Returning)?
5. Which product categories generate the highest estimated profit?
6. How do payment methods perform across online and retail sales channels?
7. Which sales channel (Online vs Retail) contributes more to revenue?

## Project Process

1. Data Understanding
    
   Reviewed the dataset to understand available fields such as sales amount, quantity, region, product category, sales channel, payment method, customer type, and time.
2. Data Cleaning and Preparation
    - Ensured consistent data types and formatting
    - Validated categorical fields (region, product category, sales channel)
    - Verified numerical fields for sales and profit calculations
3. Data Modeling
     - Created calculated measures for total sales, quantity sold, and estimated profit
     - Built relationships (where applicable) to support accurate aggregations  
4. Dashboard Design
     - Selected appropriate visualizations for each business question
     - Applied a consistent color theme for clarity and readability
     - Arranged visuals in a logical layout for intuitive analysis
5. Insights & Validation
      - Cross-checked visuals against raw data
      - Ensured KPIs and charts accurately reflect business performance

## Interactive Slicers

Users can interactively refine insights through:

   - Product Category
   - Sales Channel

## Key Insights
   - Sales performance varies significantly across regions, with one region leading overall revenue.
   - Certain sales representatives consistently outperform others.
   - Returning customers contribute slightly more to total sales than new customers.
   - Sales show noticeable seasonal trends across months.
   - Product categories contribute almost evenly to estimated profit, indicating diversified revenue streams.
